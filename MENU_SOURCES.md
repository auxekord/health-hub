## What is and is not reproduced

Only factual item names, categories, selected standard-serving numeric facts and provenance are included. No prices, menu photographs, full promotional descriptions, location availability results or claimed license grants are invented. Public access does not itself establish permission to reproduce an entire publication. These limited factual selections include source attribution and require normal rights review if expanded into a commercial menu database. No restricted third-party menu database is used.

Fixins' published PDF is explicitly Detroit-specific. Sexy Steak uses the confirmed Detroit domain, not the parked candidate domain. Zukin's menu/footer identifies Lincoln Park. National chains are not represented as verified individual branches.

## Nutrition limitations

McDonald's numeric selections are limited to explicit public prose and two protein image-alt labels; dynamic internal nutrition endpoints are not queried or inferred. Chick-fil-A fixed sandwiches use the visible Amount per serving table. Added sugar is not supplied on those tables and remains unknown. Variable sizes, customizable bowls, meal bundles and ambiguous defaults remain names-only even where the page displays a number. All allergen information remains conservatively incomplete for safety/cross-contact assurance; explicit published contains statements are retained where checked. No vegetarian, vegan or religious certification is inferred from ingredients or marketing.

Independent restaurant nutrients remain unknown. User-entered/reference estimates may be added privately in the editor with their own source, never labeled official automatically. Test-only fictional publishers/items are excluded from application and preview data.

Dunkin' remains unverified because the official menu/PDF denied access. This does not prevent expansion of other public menus. No TLS verification, access restriction or credential boundary was bypassed.
