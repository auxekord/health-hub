# Verified menu source register

**Verified menu publications in this build: none. Verified menu items: zero.**

Normal HTTPS access to candidate sources was denied by the development environment proxy. See RESTAURANT_COVERAGE.md for every attempted address and unresolved restaurant identity. Candidate links are not confirmed official sources and are not imported as menus.

The production/demo catalog data file, `app/catalog-data.js`, contains five explicitly unverified user-requested directory entries, no source publications and no menu items. The browser makes no background requests to these restaurants.

A future reviewed source entry must include:

- Stable source ID, publisher and official/authorized/reference classification.
- Exact HTTPS publication URL, region/location applicability and standard serving basis.
- Review date and evidence notes identifying the relevant menu/nutrition/dietary publication.
- Permission/attribution notes; review licensing before copying vendor content.
- Separate references for identity, menu names, nutrition and dietary declarations when they come from different publications.

Independent restaurant menus may be included with all nutrients unknown after actual menu verification. Reference-food estimates require their own source and assumptions and cannot be relabeled official. Current tests use fictional test publications and items exclusively; those fixtures are excluded from the demo ZIP and are not entries in this register.

Source access or user-supplied official publications are required before claiming actual menu browsing or restaurant-specific recommendations. No access controls were bypassed and no paid provider was enabled.
